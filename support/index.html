FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Nothing is sent to a server — your financial information never leaves your phone. RentLedger does not operate servers or maintain user accounts.

Your RentLedger data is included in your iCloud or local device backups automatically. As long as you have device backups enabled in your iPhone settings, your data is safe.

You can export your payments, expenses, and vendors as CSV files from Settings → Export Data. Choose how to share the file — email it to your accountant, save to Files or iCloud Drive, or send via AirDrop. Schedule E summaries also export as PDF from the Reports tab.

Open RentLedger, go to Settings, and tap "Delete All Data." This permanently removes all payments, expenses, properties, tenants, vendors, and receipt files from your device. This action cannot be undone.

New subscribers receive a free trial with full access to every feature. After the trial, you can continue with a monthly ($4.99/mo) or annual ($39.99/yr) subscription, or make a one-time lifetime purchase ($79.99). Current trial length is shown on the in-app paywall.

Yes. RentLedger offers a one-time lifetime purchase ($79.99) that unlocks all features and includes all future updates — no recurring charges, no renewals, no surprises.

Open RentLedger, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." This will restore any active subscription or lifetime purchase linked to your Apple ID. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, find RentLedger, and tap Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.
