Billing FAQ

Common pricing questions

Product questions? See the full support FAQ.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — new subscribers get a free trial with full access to every feature. Start the trial from within the app. Cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged. The current trial length is shown on the in-app paywall.

Which plan should I pick?

Monthly if you only use RentLedger at tax time (Feb–April) and cancel the rest of the year. Annual if you track year-round and want the 33% discount over monthly billing. Lifetime if you plan to own rental property for at least 2 years — it breaks even vs Annual after year 2 and ends billing forever.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade from Monthly to Annual via your Apple ID subscription settings at any time. Switching from a subscription to Lifetime is a one-time purchase — after you buy Lifetime, you can cancel your active subscription and keep full access forever.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RentLedger → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

What happens if I cancel — do I lose my data?

No. Your rental data stays on your device regardless of subscription status. Premium features (receipt scanning, tax reports, CSV export) lock until you renew or purchase Lifetime, but everything you've logged remains intact and exportable.

How do I restore a purchase on a new iPhone?

Your subscription or Lifetime purchase is tied to your Apple ID. On a new iPhone, install RentLedger, open Settings in the app, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
