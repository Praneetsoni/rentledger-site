        <div data-reveal-group>
          <details><summary><span>What does the free trial include?</span><span class="chev"></span></summary><p>Every feature is unlocked during the trial. You're not charged until it ends, and you can cancel anytime in Apple ID settings.</p></details>
          <details><summary><span>How do I cancel?</span><span class="chev"></span></summary><p>Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → RentLedger → Cancel. Your data stays on your device when a subscription lapses.</p></details>
          <details><summary><span>Can I switch plans later?</span><span class="chev"></span></summary><p>Yes. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase that replaces any active subscription.</p></details>
          <details><summary><span>How do I restore a purchase on a new iPhone?</span><span class="chev"></span></summary><p>Your subscription or Lifetime purchase is tied to your Apple ID. On a new iPhone, install RentLedger, open Settings in the app, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.</p></details>
          <details><summary><span>What does "Lifetime" actually mean?</span><span class="chev"></span></summary><p>One payment, every future update included, forever. No renewals. No billing emails. If we ever raise prices, your Lifetime stays Lifetime.</p></details>
          <details><summary><span>Do you offer refunds?</span><span class="chev"></span></summary><p>Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We don't process payments directly, so we can't issue them ourselves.</p></details>
